Set up your event in four steps

Designed for a volunteer doing this the night before with no tech expertise needed.

1

Enter your constraints

Teams, pitches, match length, start time, hard end time. Takes about 3 minutes.

2

Pick your format

The AI suggests 2–3 options in plain English — pros, cons, estimated finish time. You choose, Féile generates the draw.

3

Share the registration link

Drop one link in your WhatsApp group. Managers open it and see everything they need — no sign-up required.

4

Run the day

Enter scores on your phone. Standings update live for everyone. Send the public link and let the day run itself.

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Treoir, your AI co-organiser

Treoir recommends the right format — and explains why

Treoir is the AI built into every tournament, from setup to the final whistle. Just fill in your constraints and it returns 2–3 format options with plain-English trade-offs — no sports management experience required. It feasibility-checks each option against your actual numbers so nothing gets recommended that can't fit.

  • Green / amber / red feasibility at a glance
  • Estimated finish time per option
  • Round Robin, Groups + Knockout, Blitz and more

Treoir — Format Suggestion

Groups of 4 + Cup & Shield Recommended

4–6 games per team · Finishes ~14:45

Every team plays at least 4 games. Clear progression path. Works perfectly with 3 pitches and a 15:00 finish.

Full Round Robin Tight

6 games per team · Finishes ~15:20 (over)

More games but risks running over. Consider if finals aren't required.

The bit no spreadsheet can do

Treoir stays with you on the day — it has live access to fixtures, scores and standings. Ask it anything, and it doesn't just answer, it acts: mark a walkover, reshuffle a group, or tell you what to cut when you're running behind.

  • Marks walkovers and reshuffles groups instantly
  • Recommends what to cut when you're behind schedule
  • Drafts WhatsApp and social messages, ready to send

Full schedule generated in seconds

Féile builds a complete fixture list — pitch allocation, kick-off times, rest intervals. It flags conflicts before they cause problems on the day, then lets you drag-and-drop to adjust anything you want.

  • Conflict warnings for back-to-back fixtures
  • Drag-and-drop to swap fixtures or teams
  • Export as PDF by pitch or by team

Go Games sorted from a photo, not a spreadsheet

County boards send round schedules as an image — host club, visiting clubs, how many teams each is bringing. Upload it and Féile reads it, creates every team, and builds a fair, cross-club fixture list automatically. No re-typing club names every fortnight, no working out who plays who by hand.

  • Upload the county board's schedule photo — clubs and teams created for you
  • Fair cross-club fixtures, capped games per team
  • Built for how Go Games actually runs — no scores, no finals, no stress

Sideline Stats Coming soon

It never loses your match

Sideline Stats is for whoever is holding the phone on the sideline. Track Gaelic football, ladies’ football, hurling or camogie with a tap per score, and turn every match into a graphic for the club’s socials — no design work, no rebuilding the match in Canva that evening.

  • Every tap is saved the moment it happens, so a closed app or a flat battery doesn’t lose the match
  • One tap for a point, a wide or a card, and a clock you can set to the referee’s
  • Scorers named from your own squad, and anything corrected on the timeline
  • A full-time graphic with the scorers and your club’s crest, ready to share
  • No signal, no account, and nothing collected: matches stay on your phone

Sideline Coach Coming soon

Plan the session. Show them the move.

Sideline Coach is for volunteer coaches of grassroots youth football. Plan each session as stations another coach could pick up and run, draw match-day shapes on a tactics board, and keep a note of how each session and match went.

  • Sessions built from stations, each with its own set-up and coaching points
  • Run the session pitchside, with a timer to rotate the groups
  • Draw a move on the tactics board, then play it back — players follow movement far better than arrows
  • No account and nothing collected: your plans stay on your phone
