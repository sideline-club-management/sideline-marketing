Delete your Sideline account

One Sideline account signs you in to Sideline Stats and Sideline Féile. Deleting it deletes it for both, along with everything stored with it.

In the app, straight away

  1. Sideline Stats: open Settings, then Sync, and tap Delete your Sideline account. Confirm, and it is deleted at once.
  2. Sideline Féile: open Account settings, choose Delete account under Danger zone, type DELETE and confirm.

You need to be signed in to do it in the app. If you can’t sign in any more, or no longer have the app, use the email below instead.

By email, without the app

Email privacy@sideline.community from the address you sign in with, and ask for your Sideline account to be deleted. If you write from another address, say which one the account uses; we will check with that address before deleting anything. Your account and everything listed below is deleted within one month, and we reply to say it has been done.

What is deleted

  • Your account: your email address and the record that lets you sign in.
  • Everything Sideline Stats has synced: each of your devices’ encrypted copy of your matches, squads and club, and the locked key that opens them.
  • In Sideline Féile: the clubs you created, their tournaments, and those tournaments’ teams, fixtures, results, standings and uploaded documents.

None of it is kept afterwards. The database has no backups that would hold a copy, so once it is deleted it is gone, and it cannot be undone.

What is not deleted

  • Matches on your phone or tablet. Sideline Stats keeps every match on the device it is on, and deleting the account leaves them there. To remove them, delete the app.
  • Backup files you have made, wherever you saved or sent them. Delete those yourself.
  • Clubs in Sideline Féile that someone else created, even if you helped run them.

The servers that run sign-in keep routine logs of requests for a short time, as any server does, and those age out on their own.
