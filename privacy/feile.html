Sideline Féile

What a tournament needs, and nothing more

Féile stores organisers, teams, fixtures and results. It holds no information about players, and no contact details for team managers.

Féile holds no information about players. Running a tournament needs organisers, teams, fixtures and results — so that is what it stores. Nobody’s child is in the database, and neither is a team manager’s phone number or email address.

What is stored

If you organise

  • Your email address, used to sign you in. There is no password — signing in sends a link to your email. Sign-in is by invitation: an account can only be created for an address that a club or Sideline has invited.
  • Your club’s name, and the name and email address of anyone you invite to help run it. The invitation is emailed to them.
  • The tournaments you create: name, dates, venues, pitches, age group, format, and the contact details you choose to publish on the tournament’s page.
  • Any schedule sheet or document you upload, including a photo or PDF of a county board’s schedule that you ask the app to read (see below), and what the app read from it.
  • If you use the AI organiser, Treoir: the conversation you have with it, saved against your account.
  • For each referee score link you make: the name you gave it (for example “Mark”), the pitch it covers, the results entered through it and when it was last used.

If you enter a team

  • The club name, and an optional team name.
  • Whether the entry is confirmed and whether an entry fee has been paid. Payments themselves are not taken through the app.

Once it is running

  • Fixtures, kick-off times, pitch allocations, scores and standings.

If you referee

A referee scores from a link the organiser sends, with no account and no email address. The link carries the name the organiser gave it. While you score, your phone keeps a random ID, so the organiser can see whether a link is open on more than one phone. It holds no name or contact details, and a scoring session ends after 14 hours.

What is public

A tournament has a public page so players, parents and clubs can follow it without an account. That page shows the fixtures, times, pitches, team names, scores and standings, along with whatever contact details the organiser chose to publish. Teams are shown by club and team name only; there are no manager contact details to show.

Who else processes it

  • Supabase hosts the database, the sign-in system and the server-side functions. Everything described above is stored there.
  • Anthropic is used only in two places. When you ask the app to read a county board’s schedule or group draw from a photo or PDF, that image or file is sent to Anthropic’s API to be turned into fixtures. When you use Treoir, the AI organiser, your conversation is sent along with a summary of the tournament you have open: its name, date, venue, format, fixtures, scores and standings, and, once it is over, the names of the clubs that took part. The app adds no email addresses or phone numbers to it.
  • Resend delivers the email inviting someone to join your club. It is sent to the address you entered, with the invited person’s name, your club’s name and the link to accept.

As with any website, the servers that deliver it keep standard logs — IP address, browser type, pages requested and the time — which the hosting provider records automatically. There is no analytics service, no advertising network, and no tracking of any kind.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Organisers can delete their own account from within the app, which removes the account and the clubs it owns. For anything else — including if you want a team entry removed — get in touch and it will be dealt with within one month.

Children

Féile is used to run youth tournaments, but it is a tool for the adults running them. It holds no names, dates of birth or any other personal information about players. A team is recorded as a club name and an optional team name, with no contact details attached.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Niall McKinney. Questions about how information is handled, or requests to see, correct or remove it, go to privacy@sideline.community.
