Support

Stuck with Sideline Stats, Sideline Féile or Sideline Coach, or found something wrong? Email us and a person will reply.

Email support@sideline.community

We aim to reply within a few days. Sideline is run by volunteers, so it may be slower around a busy weekend of fixtures.

What helps us help you

  • Which app, and whether it’s on a phone or a tablet, iPhone or Android.
  • The app’s version. In Sideline Stats it’s at the foot of Settings.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot is often the quickest way to show it.
  • A wrong score in Sideline Stats: the code and grade, and the minute it went wrong, if you know it.

For a problem with a Sideline Stats match, a backup file of it can help us see exactly what happened. Only send one if you’re comfortable to: it includes your squad’s names. Sharing the match’s timeline as text, or a screenshot, is often enough.

Your data and your account

  • Questions about what the apps store, or a request for a copy of your data: privacy@sideline.community, and the privacy policies.
  • To delete your Sideline account: how to delete your Sideline account.

Ideas and feedback

Something you’d like the apps to do? Tell us through the feedback form, or email support.
